Our story

Our Mission & History

A non-profit archery education center built around learning, safety, inclusion, and community.

Founded in 2005

Helping the Community Discover Archery

Straight to the Point Archery is a non-profit archery education center founded in 2005 by Giap Nguyen.

We aim to create a safe, welcoming environment where students can develop focus, confidence, discipline, and sportsmanship through archery.

Our Facilities

28 Indoor Shooting Lanes

14 ten-yard lanes and 14 twenty-yard lanes.

Private Coaching Rooms

Two rooms for personalized instruction.

Outdoor Shooting Field

Access to 26 additional twenty-yard lanes and an outdoor shooting field.

Programs for Our Community

Programs include lessons, tournaments, parties, homeschool programs, kids' club, YMCA, and Scout programs.

Inclusive and Respectful Learning

We are committed to a non-discriminatory environment where every student is treated with respect.
